Future And Managed Ideas

This page separates what exists now from what we want to build next. The hardest problems (fleet blast radius, governed promotion, and live-to-desired reconciliation) are real, but not all fully proven here.

Use it as a roadmap boundary: useful direction, not a replacement for current evidence.

Guardrails

Do not describe planned ideas as shipped behavior. The public experiment uses evidence-backed words: pass, watch, blocked, refused, not applicable, and planned.

That honesty is part of the product story: expert users need to know which claims are strong today and which still need more work.
