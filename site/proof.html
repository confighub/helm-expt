Proof, not promises.

Generated at: 2026-06-13T10:09:53.911Z UTC · source: committed helm-expt evidence for this proof page.

This page explains what the catalog proves, what each lane means, and where the proof stops. It is intentionally narrower than a marketing claim.

Render parity is the starting point. The stronger claim is staged: exact objects, ConfigHub Units, scans, live apply, GitOps/OCI reconciliation, semantic comparison with Helm, lifecycle routing, and observation receipts.

Serious Charts Are The Test

The hard cases are where the product has to earn trust: kube-prometheus-stack, cert-manager, External Secrets, Argo Workflows, Argo Rollouts, stateful databases, and charts with hooks, CRDs, webhooks, generated secrets, storage, or target facts.

For these charts, a green render is not enough. The proof must say which prerequisites are required, which lifecycle route is selected, what the target observed, and whether the production scope is accepted, superseded, rejected, or still under review.
