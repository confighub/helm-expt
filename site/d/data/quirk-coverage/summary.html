How To Use This

  • Use tracked-and-surfaced axes in user-facing chart status and pain reports.
  • Treat partly-tracked axes as visible, but not complete enough for a broad production claim.
  • Treat source-scanned-not-surfaced axes as cheap next candidates for chart-facts.csv or variant-path coverage.
  • Treat not-scanned axes as known blind spots. A chart may still be usable, but the project should not imply that this behavior was checked.
