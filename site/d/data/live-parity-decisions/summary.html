How To Read One Decision

residue_category is the kind of gap (gitops-runtime, operate-policy, target-runtime, target-prerequisite, semantic-model-gap); blocker_owner is who acts; usable_today is the honest answer for a Helm user; user_decision is the plain explanation; next_action is the concrete step; support_artifact is the recipe file that records the decision; receipt is the committed evidence.

Boundaries

  • Read-only classification of committed G/P-lane evidence. No live run, no cluster, no runs/ receipt edited, nothing written into data/live-helm-confighub-compare/, and no change to the master-matrix or status-dashboard generators.
  • A decision never turns a watch into a pass or a failure; it explains the residue and routes the fix.
