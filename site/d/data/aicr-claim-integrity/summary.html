What this does not check

It checks numbers, not meaning. A page can count correctly and still describe the wrong thing, and this lane will pass it. The claims that resist counting, such as which rung an entry has climbed or what a proof did not prove, are held by the receipts and by the platform evidence record rather than here.

Everything runs offline against committed bytes. No cluster, no organization, and no network takes part.
