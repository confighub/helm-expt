Hooks and other actions in ConfigHub deployments

Some Helm charts use hooks or other lifecycle steps. We show those steps instead of hiding them.

A chart page tells you whether an action is observed, routed, per-target, blocked, refused, or still needs a recipe. A route is useful guidance; it is not an automatic execution claim unless the route says so and evidence proves it.
