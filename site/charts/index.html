Hooks and other actions in ConfigHub deployments

In Helm, some work appears as hooks or implicit lifecycle behavior. In the ConfigHub model, that work should become explicit actions: preflight checks, target-owned prerequisites, Argo/Flux sync actions, post-apply observations, or refused routes. A chart page tells you whether an action is observed, routed, per-target, blocked, refused, or still needs a recipe. A route is useful guidance; it is not an automatic execution claim unless the route says so and evidence proves it.
