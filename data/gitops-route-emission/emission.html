Argo CD hook snippet

Add to the route's rendered hook object (example: a PostSync run-job):

metadata:
  annotations:
    argocd.argoproj.io/hook: PostSync
    argocd.argoproj.io/hook-delete-policy: HookSucceeded
Routes your cluster already handles (ordering, cleanup, webhook cert) and inputs you supply (target facts) emit nothing — shown as none. Emitting a route as your controller's native hook keeps it visible and audited, with no new runtime — the bridge to product-direct execution (#688).
