The cub-direct (no-controller) path applies the OCI bundle with plain `kubectl apply`, which has no dependency ordering or wait. On a FIRST install of a chart that ships a CRD plus a CR of that CRD, the CR fails ('no matches for kind ... ensure CRDs are installed first') and is never created. Helm installs CRDs first; Argo (sync waves) and Flux (retry/health) order it; cub-direct does not.
